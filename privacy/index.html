Privacy information

Last updated: October 13, 2026.

Little Home Ideas is currently a static informational website. The patio privacy idea finder runs locally in your browser. The values entered into that tool are not sent to us by its code.

This website is served through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request data to deliver the site, maintain security and operate its services. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Analytics and advertising

We have not intentionally installed third-party analytics, advertising trackers or affiliate scripts in this initial website version. This page must be updated before introducing any of those services.

Cookies

The website's own HTML and JavaScript do not currently set cookies. Hosting providers may use necessary technical mechanisms outside our code.

Contact and operator information

Operator identification and a dedicated contact address have not yet been published. This is an interim information page, not a final legal privacy notice. The site operator must provide the applicable legal details before full public commercial launch.

Saved ideas on your device

If you choose to save a guide, its identifier is stored in your browser's local storage so you can find it again on this device. Saved ideas are not sent to us, linked to an account, or synchronized between devices. Remove an idea with its bookmark button or clear this website's storage in your browser settings.
