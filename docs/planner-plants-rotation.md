# Plants, seating rotation, and step styling

The furniture section also offers 0–6 potted plants, with thuja-style and leafy illustrations. Footprint width includes the pot and foliage allowance; total height includes the pot. Plants use the same drag/keyboard controls and conservative rectangular collision footprints as furniture. New plants find open floor positions; existing compatible poses remain on recalculation. Plant shapes are illustrations, not botanical, climate, growth, or product recommendations.

Selected chairs and benches rotate in 90-degree steps using left/right buttons or R (Shift+R for left). Rotation changes the original seat geometry and, for rectangular seats, swaps footprint dimensions. Center-based rotation is constrained to the floor; overlaps are rejected without losing the current layout. Rotations and plant dimensions/positions are included in saved worksheets. The selected-item picker makes every piece available without precise pointer targeting.

Floor/roof, boundaries/screens, and furniture/plants have separate tinted cards, numbered headings, and distinct borders. Existing conditional fields and optional panel estimates remain.

Validation covers plants with and without furniture, valid placement and collision checks, plant height against roof height, rectangular seating rotation, rejected overlapping rotations, pose persistence when adding plants, toolbar enablement, keyboard rotation and plant movement.
