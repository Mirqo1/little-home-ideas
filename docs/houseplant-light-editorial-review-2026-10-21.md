# Indoor window-light guide — October 21, 2026

## Intent and source review

US searches for houseplant light at windows surfaced extension guides and commercial plant guides. No keyword volume, difficulty, traffic, or product-performance data was obtained. Our contribution is a location card and repeatable observation worksheet that separates direct sunlight observations, window position, ordinary curtain use, weather, and gaps in the record. This is a planning guide, not a ranking of plants or a calibrated measurement method.

Primary sources opened and reviewed:

- University of Minnesota Extension, Lighting for Indoor Plants and Starting Seeds: assess available natural light before plant selection; overhangs can interrupt a nominally sunny exposure.
- Iowa State University Extension, How to Care for Houseplants, Light section: light at a plant depends on window direction, size, distance, coverings, and outdoor obstructions; bright indirect light definition. Unrelated general health and air-quality claims were not adopted.
- University of Maryland Extension, Lighting for Indoor Plants: intensity, duration and quality are distinct; weather, seasonal and physical shading factors; supplemental light needs appropriate selection.

## Quality gate

- The exact proposed leaf position is the observation point. Example distances are records, not plant-care prescriptions.
- Morning/midday/afternoon are checkpoints, not an exact duration estimate. Missed periods remain unknown; repeat observations do not guarantee suitability.
- Source light-category numerical ranges differ; no universal numeric category table or phone-meter accuracy claims were copied.
- No universal species recommendations, watering timetable, or diagnosis from a light log. Care remains specific to plant identity and container.
- Two original ImageGen illustrations; prompts and resized WebP hashes retained. No generated image presented as a measured experiment or completed client project.
- Date October 21, American English, relevant incoming link from container gardening and outgoing links to gardening and room layout. No affiliate links or fabricated product testing.
