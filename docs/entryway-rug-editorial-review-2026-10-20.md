## Intent and contribution

US search spot check: “rug door clearance measure rug pad door entryway rug thickness.” Existing results include Rugs Direct's entryway sizing guide, manufacturer pad guides, and buying guides focused on clearance. This is a competitive topic; no search volume or ranking-difficulty claim was obtained. Our contribution is a product-neutral record joining the swept footprint, smallest observed gap, total rug/edge height, separate or integrated pad, care feasibility, and return conditions. It compares keeping the rug under the door with moving it beyond the swing. No claim of firsthand installation or product testing.
