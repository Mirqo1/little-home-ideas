Doors & windows on existing walls

Select a wall above to add an opening.

Drag doors along their wall; drag windows along the wall and up/down. Symbols start at up to 3 ft wide; door height up to 6.5 ft, window height up to 3 ft. Smaller wall sections reduce the symbol size. Existing-opening markers only; no construction, door swing, or access assessment.

Measure from the floor to the top you want, even when screening starts above a railing. A point or comma works for decimals.

What the drawing means

A rectangular floor, a flat full-area roof, wall/railing sections on four sides, example screen modules, furniture/plant footprints, and movable existing-door/window markers. Partial walls can share a side with a privacy screen, railing, or both. Screens span the available section unless you choose a shorter length; separate pieces can leave regular or uneven gaps. Product panel counts are optional and never approve cutting or fitting a panel.

The planner checks geometric fit, furniture overlap, and opening overlap on each wall. It does not assess walking clearance, door swings, wind, structural loads, guard compliance, or mounting methods. Confirm product and building requirements before installation. Read our patio privacy guide.
