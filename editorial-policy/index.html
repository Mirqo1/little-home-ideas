Our editorial approach

We want home improvement ideas to be useful, clear about limitations, and practical for real budgets and living situations.

What we aim to publish

Step-by-step explanations, comparisons, design inspiration and small interactive tools. We distinguish general suggestions from claims that require measurements, expert review or real-world testing.

Safety first

Projects involving structural loads, electricity, plumbing, elevated balconies, building codes or other significant hazards may require permission or professional guidance. Our content is not a substitute for qualified advice.

Product and price claims

We do not describe products as personally tested unless that testing actually occurred. Indicative prices, when included, should be dated and supported by current sources. Readers should verify availability and compatibility independently.

AI-assisted drafting

Drafting and research may be assisted by AI. Material facts, safety claims, sources and editorial quality should be reviewed before publication. We will correct significant errors when identified.

Advertising and affiliate relationships

We currently have no affiliate links or advertisements in this initial version. If these are added, we will clearly disclose commercial relationships and keep editorial recommendations separate from paid placements.

Images and illustrations

Some decorative images, including the homepage home-and-patio scene, are created with AI. They illustrate ideas and do not document a real project, product test, or before-and-after result. Our planning diagrams are original illustrations and are not to scale. We do not use generated images as evidence of a tested result.
